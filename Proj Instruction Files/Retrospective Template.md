# Project – Retrospective

Each individual will answer these questions relative to your project. Please provided thoughtful answers that provide insight to your contributions and your team dynamics. Submit a Word or PDF document on Blazeview.

What worked well for your team? (e.g. What practices or decisions helped you succeed? Where did teamwork/communication work especially well? What exceeded expectations?, etc.)

What did not work well for your team? (e.g. Where did we struggle or lose time? What caused frustration or confusion? What risks/issues did we miss? etc.)

Were you to continue with another sprint, what should your team do to improve its process (e.g. stop doing, start doing, continue doing, etc.)
